# How to Redo a Single Floor's Manual Route

## ✅ Good News!
You **don't need to start over** from Floor 1. You can redo just the 2nd floor while keeping your Floor 1 work intact!

## Method 1: Delete Saved Path (Recommended)

### Steps:
1. **Go to Floor 2** with BFO→MISSO selected
2. **Enable "Manual Route" toggle**
3. **Look for the orange warning box** that says:
   ```
   ⚠️ Floor 2 already has a saved path [Delete]
   ```
4. **Click "Delete"** to remove only Floor 2's saved path
5. **Confirm** the deletion
6. **Draw new points** on Floor 2
7. **Click "Save manual route"**

### What Happens:
- ✅ Floor 1's path remains **unchanged**
- ✅ Floor 2's old path is **deleted**
- ✅ Floor 2's new path is **saved**
- ✅ Navigation uses Floor 1 (old) + Floor 2 (new)

---

## Method 2: Clear Draft and Redraw

### Steps:
1. **Go to Floor 2** with BFO→MISSO selected
2. **Enable "Manual Route" toggle**
3. **Click "Clear floor"** to remove any unsaved draft points
4. **Draw new points** on Floor 2 by long-pressing
5. **Click "Save manual route"**

### What Happens:
- ✅ Clears unsaved draft points on Floor 2
- ✅ You can redraw immediately
- ⚠️ **Note**: This only clears the current editing session, not the saved route

---

## Method 3: Complete Redo of Both Floors

### Steps:
1. **Go to Floor 1** with BFO→MISSO selected
2. **Enable "Manual Route" toggle**
3. **Delete Floor 1's saved path** (if needed)
4. **Delete Floor 2's saved path** (if needed)
5. **Draw Floor 1 path** → Switch to Floor 2
6. **Draw Floor 2 path** → Save

### What Happens:
- ✅ Complete fresh start
- ✅ Both floors get new paths

## Quick Answer to Your Question:

**Question**: Can I redo just Floor 2, or do I need to start from Floor 1?

**Answer**: ✅ **You can redo JUST Floor 2!**

### Quickest Method:
1. Navigate to Floor 2
2. Select BFO as Start, MISSO as Destination
3. Enable "Manual Route"
4. Click "Delete" in the orange warning box
5. Draw your new path
6. Click "Save manual route"

Your Floor 1 path will remain untouched! 🎉

---

## Floor Status Indicators

The chips show you what's saved:
- **Green checkmark** (✓): Floor has a saved path
- **Gray circle** (○): Floor has no saved path
- **Blue background**: Currently viewing this floor

This helps you see which floors need work without switching between them!
